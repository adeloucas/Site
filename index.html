Assyriologist · Digital Humanist · Ancient Historian

I study Bronze Age Mesopotamian cities and their civic, economic, and legal institutions. I also work on provenance records of American cuneiform collections prior to 1970.

I am not accepting consultation requests at this time.

Research

Ongoing · 2026–

Provenance of American Cuneiform Collections

I am tracing the history of American cuneiform collections prior to 1970, focusing on private acquisition and institutional exchanges.

Dissertation · 2023–2026

Elite Persistence in Old Babylonian Nippur & Ur (2026-1700 BCE)

I investigated the institutional economy of two Old Babylonian cities using a relational database built for 2,500 cuneiform records and 13,000 attested names.

Ongoing · 2025–

Gaming Mesopotamia

I am investigating how Mesopotamia has been represented in 'edutainment' spaces and its influence on modern cultural historical study.
